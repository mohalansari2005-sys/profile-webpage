## Who I am

I'm a software engineer based in Riyadh, currently studying Information Systems at King Saud University in the Data Science and Management track and interning at Majara as a Product Engineering Intern. My work sits at the intersection of software engineering and product development: understanding a problem, working with stakeholders to define what needs to be built, and turning those requirements into working software.

Before moving deeper into engineering, I spent a few months in business development at SEET (صيت), a Riyadh marketing agency. That experience shaped how I approach technical work today. It taught me to start with the question of what the other side actually needs rather than immediately jumping to a solution — a mindset I now apply when working with stakeholders and translating business problems into technical requirements.

Most of my hands-on engineering experience so far centers on backend systems: Python, FastAPI, SQLAlchemy, REST API design, PostgreSQL, Redis, Celery, Docker, and pytest. I'm comfortable moving from a requirements conversation toward a working system, and I care about the parts of engineering that don't always show up in a demo — idempotency, retries, multi-tenancy, data integrity, and what happens when an external dependency misbehaves.

I'm also developing my skills across the rest of the stack. I've worked with React to connect frontend interfaces to backend services, giving me a better understanding of how decisions on one side of an application affect the other. More broadly, I'm interested in becoming the kind of engineer who can move between product requirements, frontend, backend, and infrastructure rather than being limited to a single layer of the system.

AI engineering is another area I'm actively exploring. I haven't built production AI products yet, but I've been deliberately learning how LLMs, retrieval, and agent-based systems fit into real software — this portfolio's chat feature is where that learning happens in practice. It's a deliberate exception to how I normally approach projects: where I'd usually default to the simplest tool that solves the problem, here I intentionally chose a more involved architecture — retrieval over this structured knowledge corpus, agent orchestration with LangGraph, and a self-hosted backend — specifically to get hands-on experience with the engineering challenges behind these systems, not to make the portfolio itself unnecessarily complex.

More broadly, I'm interested in the space where **software engineering, product thinking, and AI intersect**. I'm still early in my career, but I'm intentionally building toward roles where I can understand ambiguous problems, communicate with both technical and non-technical stakeholders, make sound engineering decisions, and turn those decisions into systems people can actually use.
