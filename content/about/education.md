## Degree and track

I'm studying for a Bachelor's degree in Information Systems at King Saud University in Riyadh, in the **Data Science and Management** track. I started in **August 2024** and expect to graduate in **June 2028**. My GPA is **4.7 out of 5.0**.

Information Systems sits deliberately between software engineering and the business side of building software. The Data Science and Management track leans that further toward working with data and toward the organisational questions around a system — who uses it, what decisions it supports, and what it needs to fit into — rather than treating a system purely as code. That framing matches the kind of engineering work I gravitate to: understanding a problem and the people who have it, then turning that understanding into something that runs.

## Future Technology Club (FTC)

I'm a member and a team leader at the **Future Technology Club (FTC)**, where I lead a 7-person Innovation Lab team.

The team's purpose is to explore ideas and turn the promising ones into projects that can actually be implemented across the club and the wider university. My role spans both sides of that: contributing directly to ideation, problem-solving, and project development, and coordinating the team so initiatives keep moving from an idea toward execution rather than stalling as concepts.

It is the same progression I care about in engineering work — the distance between a loosely defined idea and a working thing is where most of the real effort sits — applied in a setting where the people involved are volunteers and the projects have to earn their place.
